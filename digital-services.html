Digital Services

CCM is building digital infrastructure to support its ministries and departments. Some systems are internal-only; this page describes them at a high level for transparency, without exposing access to the public.

Built on Microsoft 365

CCM's internal systems are built around Microsoft 365. This portal's sign-in is designed to connect to that same identity system for staff and leaders.

What's public vs. internal

  • This portal — public
  • Ministry & department information — public
  • Volunteer resources — public
  • CompassionOS — staff & leaders only
