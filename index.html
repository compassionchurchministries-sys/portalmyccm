Welcome to Compassion Church Ministries

Compassion Church Ministries exists to win souls for the Kingdom of Jesus Christ. We do this by building thriving, compassionate, and engaged communities of disciples. Following Jesus himself as an example of service, we are devoted to serving our community through educational centers for children and adults with meaningful programs focused on juvenile delinquency, mental health, and entrepreneurship.

About the organization

Church, ministry, and community — organized to grow.

Compassion Church Ministries operates as a church organization made up of distinct ministries and departments, led by pastors, directors, and volunteer leaders working together. As CCM has grown, so has the need for shared infrastructure — this portal, and the digital services behind it, exist to support that.
