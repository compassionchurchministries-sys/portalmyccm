About Compassion Church Ministries

CCM is a church organization made up of ministries, departments, leaders, and volunteers working together — with a growing set of digital tools connecting it all.

Church, ministry, and organization

Compassion Church Ministries exists as a church community first — but running that community well takes real organization. CCM is structured around ministries that serve the congregation directly, departments that keep the organization operating, and leaders and volunteers who carry both forward.

Growth and digital infrastructure

As CCM has grown, its ministries and departments have grown with it. This portal is part of a broader digital infrastructure effort — connecting the public organization to internal tools like CompassionOS and AIMS, and eventually to volunteer and member services built on the same foundation.

Ministries and departments

Two structures, working together

CCM intentionally separates ministries from departments, since not every operating unit faces the congregation the same way.

Ministries

Congregation-facing areas of service — Worship, Kids, Youth, Media, and more. Ministries are where members and volunteers connect directly with the church community.

Departments

Operating units that keep CCM running — including some, like Administration, IT, and Facilities, that don't correspond to a public-facing ministry at all.
