Serve at CCM

Volunteers are at the center of every CCM ministry. You don't need a church staff account to get started — just an interest in serving.

Find an opportunity

Every ministry at CCM lists its own volunteer roles. Browse the ministry directory, or use the form below to express interest and a ministry leader will follow up.

What to expect

  • Browse opportunities by ministry, no account required
  • Express interest through this page
  • A ministry leader follows up directly
  • Access volunteer resources and training materials
  • Eventually: view schedules and submit requests online

Looking ahead

CCM plans to offer a dedicated volunteer experience — separate from staff and leadership accounts — so volunteers can manage their own schedule and information without needing a CCM Microsoft 365 identity. Not yet available.
